# Media pilot

The pilot is manual and has no scheduler. The local implementation is not deployed. It uses the existing workspace AI admission and settlement ledger, source/grant checks, and the live MTProto account; it never creates a session, sends messages or marks messages read. Only Telegram photos are downloaded, in memory, at most 5 MiB each. Unsupported, protected or expired media remain for review. Image-derived names, contacts and evidence are unverified; candidates are pending review, carry an image-evidence risk, and are never merged solely by name.

After an explicitly authorized release and paid-pilot approval, authenticate as an authorized workspace user. Select 20–30 observations from `GET /api/telegram-crm/lead-discovery/feed?sourceId=<sourceId>&limit=50`, choosing `MANUAL_REVIEW_MEDIA` entries from the permitted source. Send `POST /api/telegram-crm/lead-discovery/sources/<sourceId>/media-pilot` with JSON `{"observationIds":["<observation-id>","..."]}`. It handles at most 30 IDs and returns observation links and unverified vision results. `GET /api/telegram-crm/lead-discovery/sources/<sourceId>/media-pilot` returns saved results. Documents and unprocessable photos do not consume a provider call. A retry verifies the photo fingerprint and reuses the durable result; UNKNOWN/RUNNING operations remain blocked.

Admission holds the existing workspace advisory lock and sums settled actual costs plus full reservations for unresolved operations for that workspace and source. It refuses a new operation if this total plus its maximum reservation exceeds $1, or if 30 pilot operations already exist. The cap spans retries and concurrent requests while the ledger records are retained (90 days); it is not an ongoing recurring budget. Existing workspace and category budgets also apply. Settlement uses the same lock. One manual batch may make up to 30 Telegram reads and 30 provider calls, with at most 150 MiB of raw image input; repeated calls may re-read photos to verify the fingerprint, but cannot automatically repay an existing operation. There is no idle work, additional timer, or image retention. Running this full batch for 100 channels would require up to 3,000 reads and provider calls and 15,000 MiB raw image input and is outside the pilot.

Manual QA after release: check source and grants, select the sample and inspect the cap, run the approved request, review evidence and provisional candidates against the linked posts, then repeat the same IDs and confirm no additional AI operations. Pause and revoked grants must stop dispatch. Check unknown outcomes and reconcile them instead of retrying them automatically.
